Setup

Hedge says hi, then asks five quick questions, and your budget is ready. Rough numbers are fine.

  1. Payday and pay. Monthly, weekly or every few weeks, in your own currency.
  2. Split your pay. Hedge fills in a guess for each category, and Not planned yet counts down to All planned.
  3. Just looking? Tap Try sample data and look around first.

Today

The first thing you see is what's safe to spend today. Underneath sits the month at a glance: what's left, what you're saving, and every category.

  1. Safe to spend today. Your month's money less bills and savings, shared over the days left.
  2. Savings goal. What you've set aside this month, and one tap to move the rest.
  3. Latest. Your last few spends, with anything new to check marked.

Budget

Every category with its plan and what's left, grouped by family. Budget by calendar month or from payday to payday.

  1. Bills tick to Paid once they go out, so you know they're done.
  2. Overview and insights show where the month went.
  3. Leftovers can carry into next month when you switch it on.

Entries

Your income, spending and moves this month, day by day. Pay and regular bills post themselves on their day.

  1. Coming up lists what's scheduled for the next six weeks.
  2. Search and filters find any entry in seconds.
  3. Swipe to delete, with an undo if you change your mind.

Wallets

Cash, bank, savings and cards in one place, with your net worth on top. Moving money between them is one entry.

  1. Net worth at a glance: wallets less what's owed on cards.
  2. Tap a wallet to see its income, spending, where it went and its five most recent entries.
  3. Paying a card is a move, so it never counts as spending twice.

Add after you pay

Your bank texts you after a card payment. Copy that text and Pouch does the typing. No setup, and it works with any bank.

  1. Copy the bank text. Press and hold the message, then tap Copy.
  2. Tap Paste bank text. Open Pouch and tap +. The amount and shop fill in, and if the text names your card, its wallet is picked too. A cash withdrawal becomes a move to Cash.
  3. Or ask Siri. Say "Hey Siri, log a spend in Pouch". Pouch stays closed and asks Just paid? next time you open it.

Questions

How does Pouch work out today's number?

It takes the money for your month, sets aside your bills and what you chose to save, and shares the rest over the days left. Every spend you log moves it.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You log a spend in a few taps, and that small habit is what keeps your money in mind. Pouch never asks for a bank login.

Can Pouch read my bank texts?

No. Pouch never reads your messages. When you copy a bank text yourself and tap Paste bank text, Pouch reads only that copied text, on your iPhone, to fill in the amount and shop.

Where is my data kept?

Only on your iPhone. There is no Pouch account and no Pouch server. A backup is saved once a week in the Files app.
