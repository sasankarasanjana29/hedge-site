Support

Something not working, or an idea? Email sasanka.rasanjana29@gmail.com. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have and what happened. A screenshot helps. You'll get a reply within a few days.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, so nobody can look it up or restore it for you. Pouch saves a backup once a week in the Files app (On My iPhone > Pouch), and Profile > Back up now makes one any time.

I got a new phone

Restore your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup and Pouch comes with it. Or copy a Pouch backup file to the new phone and open Profile > Restore a backup.

How do I add a spend faster?

Tap + on any tab. To have Pouch open by itself right after you pay, see Profile > Add after you pay: it sets up a Shortcut in about a minute.

The Dynamic Island isn't showing

Turn on Profile > Safe today on Lock Screen, then log a spend. It shows after a spend, not when you open the app.

How do I delete everything?

Profile > Delete all data, then hold the button. Deleting the app also removes everything.
