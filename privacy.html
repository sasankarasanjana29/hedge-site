Pouch

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026.

Your money stays on your phone

Everything you put into Pouch (accounts, entries, pots, big buys and settings) is saved only on your iPhone. There is no Pouch account and no Pouch server, so nobody else can see it, us included, except what you choose to show on widgets or share. Pouch never asks for a bank login and never connects to your bank.

The one thing that goes online

If you add income in another currency, Pouch fetches exchange rates from open.er-api.com (ExchangeRate-API), at most twice a day and only while you are online. One request returns every currency's rate against the US dollar and carries nothing about you or your money. Like any web request, it reaches that service from your internet address, which it may log under its own privacy policy. Without a connection Pouch keeps working with the last rate it saved.

What Pouch does not do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no sharing or selling of data. Pouch never asks for your contacts, photos or location. It asks for only two things, and only if you turn them on: Face ID for the lock, and notifications for the daily reminder. Both are optional, and iOS handles them on the phone.

Widgets

If you add a Pouch widget, Pouch copies the few figures it shows (safe to spend today, up to three pots and what is left, your savings goal, days logged this week) into a space on the phone that only Pouch and its widgets can read. Widgets on the Lock Screen can be seen without unlocking the phone; with Pouch's lock on, they hide the amounts.

Backups and export

Pouch saves a backup once a week in the Files app (On My iPhone > Pouch), on the phone only. Your iPhone may include Pouch in its own iCloud or computer backups, as it does for every app. Those follow your iPhone settings and Apple's privacy terms. Backups are ordinary files: anyone who can open Files on your unlocked iPhone can read them, and Pouch's lock does not cover them. Export makes a file and opens the share sheet; where it goes is up to you.

Deleting your data

Delete all data in Profile wipes everything Pouch keeps on this phone, including its backups in Files and the daily reminder. Deleting the app does the same.

Who makes Pouch
